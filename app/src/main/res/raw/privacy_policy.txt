File Recovery — Privacy policy
Last updated: 5 October 2026
Privacy contact: info@nexappra.com

Files and permissions
File Recovery searches and previews photos, videos and audio that Android allows it to read. Scanning uses file contents and metadata such as filenames, locations, media types, sizes and modification times. Media permissions are requested for the category you choose. Where Android offers selected-photo access, the app is limited to the selection you allow. Deep Scan reads folders you authorize through Android's folder picker. It does not access erased sectors or other apps' private storage.

Local processing and storage
Scanning, previews and photo repair run on your device. The app stores scan metadata for up to three recent scans in private cache, up to 100 successful-operation history entries in private app storage, and your theme/language preferences. Android may clear cached results. Image previews may also use local cache. Successful recovery and repair operations create copies in the destination you choose; they do not replace the source files.

Cloud export
Only when you select files and choose a cloud destination does the app write an original-file ZIP, including filenames and file contents, to that provider. Drive or another provider handles its own account sign-in, upload, retention and security. The app does not receive your cloud password. There is no automatic background backup of your media to our servers. A provider may finish synchronizing after the app completes its write. Review the selected provider's privacy policy before exporting sensitive files.

Premium purchases
Google Play handles subscription checkout and payment information. The app receives purchase identifiers and purchase status; it does not receive your payment-card details. When purchase verification is configured, it sends a purchase token over HTTPS to the verification service, which checks the subscription product, status, acknowledgement and expiry with Google Play. These details are used to provide Premium access and prevent invalid purchases. The verification code processes tokens for the request and does not store them in a database or deliberately log tokens or request bodies. Hosting infrastructure may process connection information, including IP addresses and request times, to operate the service. Infrastructure log retention depends on the service configuration. Google Play retains its own purchase records under its policies.

Other services and contact
The app contains no advertising or analytics SDK integration and does not sell your data. Email and WhatsApp support actions open the app you choose. Sending a support message discloses the information you include to that service and the support recipient. Do not send passwords or purchase tokens. Contact info@nexappra.com for privacy questions or deletion requests concerning support correspondence. Correspondence is used to respond to your request; contact us to request its deletion, subject to records that must be retained by law.

Your choices and deletion
You can change media permissions in Android Settings. Clear this app's storage or uninstall it to remove its local settings, scan metadata and recovery history; clear its cache to remove cached scan results. Clearing app storage also removes persisted folder grants. Copies or ZIP files already saved to a local folder or cloud provider remain under your control and must be deleted at that destination. Cancelling a subscription is handled in Google Play and is separate from removing local app data. This app does not create its own user accounts. Android backup/device transfer may copy app preferences according to your device settings; scan and recovery-history metadata are excluded from app backup.

Security and changes
Online purchase verification requires HTTPS. Media writes go through the destination selected in Android's picker; cloud providers control their own network transport and storage. Device permissions limit file access, but no storage or transmission method is risk-free. If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated. The policy displayed with the version you use should be checked together with the public policy and Google Play's Data safety information.
